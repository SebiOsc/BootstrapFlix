JEDI IN TRAINING

LUKE SKYWALKER

Luke is a young Rebel hero who begins his Jedi training under Master Yoda on Dagobah. Driven by compassion and a desire to protect his friends, he struggles to balance his instincts with the patience required by the Force. His determination leads him toward a confrontation with Darth Vader that challenges everything he believes. Luke's journey in this film marks a major step in his growth as a Jedi.

PORTRAYED BY Mark Hamill
REBEL PILOT

HAN SOLO

Han Solo is a daring pilot and former smuggler who fights alongside the Rebel Alliance. Though he often masks his feelings with sarcasm, his loyalty to Leia and his friends becomes increasingly clear. He pilots the Millennium Falcon through dangerous Imperial pursuit and risks everything to protect the people he cares about. His quick thinking and courage make him an essential member of the team.

PORTRAYED BY Harrison Ford
REBEL LEADER

PRINCESS LEIA

Leia is a fearless leader of the Rebel Alliance who refuses to surrender in the face of Imperial power. She is intelligent, determined, and skilled at making difficult decisions under pressure. While escaping the Empire with Han and Chewbacca, she shows both strategic strength and deep loyalty to her friends. Her leadership helps hold the Rebellion together during its darkest moments.

PORTRAYED BY Carrie Fisher
JEDI MASTER

YODA

Yoda is an ancient Jedi Master living in exile on the remote planet Dagobah. At first he appears unusual and playful, but his wisdom and knowledge of the Force are profound. He trains Luke to understand discipline, focus, and the dangers of fear and anger. Yoda's lessons become central to Luke's development as a Jedi.

VOICE BY Frank Oz
WOOKIEE COPILOT

CHEWBACCA

Chewbacca is Han Solo's loyal Wookiee companion and co-pilot of the Millennium Falcon. He is strong, resourceful, and fiercely protective of his friends. Even when the group is in danger, Chewie remains committed to helping them escape Imperial forces. His bond with Han is one of the film's most enduring friendships.

PERFORMED BY Peter Mayhew
CLOUD CITY LEADER

LANDO CALRISSIAN

Lando is the charming administrator of Cloud City and an old friend of Han Solo. He tries to protect his city and its people while navigating a dangerous agreement with the Empire. His choices place him in a difficult moral position, forcing him to decide where his loyalties truly lie. Lando ultimately takes action that helps the Rebels.

PORTRAYED BY Billy Dee Williams

THE ANTAGONISTS

The powerful enemies who threaten the Rebel Alliance.

SITH LORD

DARTH VADER

Darth Vader is the Empire's feared enforcer, relentlessly hunting Luke Skywalker and the Rebel Alliance. Powerful in the dark side of the Force, he uses intimidation and strength to control those around him. His personal connection to Luke leads to one of the saga's most consequential confrontations.

PERFORMED BY / VOICE BY David Prowse / James Earl Jones
BOUNTY HUNTER

BOBA FETT

Boba Fett is a skilled bounty hunter hired by Darth Vader to track down the Millennium Falcon and capture its passengers. Quiet, calculating, and equipped with specialized armor and gadgets, he proves to be a persistent threat to the Rebels. His role in capturing Han Solo makes him a memorable antagonist in Episode V.

PORTRAYED BY Jeremy Bulloch
