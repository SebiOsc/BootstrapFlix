THE STORY SO FAR

A GALAXY IN DANGER

The Empire has struck back.

After the destruction of the Death Star, Imperial forces relentlessly pursue the Rebel Alliance. Following the attack on the ice planet Hoth, Luke Skywalker travels to Dagobah to train with Jedi Master Yoda, while Han Solo and Princess Leia seek refuge in Cloud City. Their paths converge in a confrontation that will change the fate of the galaxy.

Directed by Irvin Kershner, this celebrated 1980 chapter expands the Star Wars saga with unforgettable characters, emotional stakes, and a legendary cliffhanger.
